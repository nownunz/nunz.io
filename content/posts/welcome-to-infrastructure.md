# Welcome to Infrastructure

This is where I'll be sharing my thoughts on infrastructure, systems design, and the technology that powers modern applications.

## What to Expect

I'll be writing about:

- **Cloud Architecture** - Designing scalable, resilient systems
- **DevOps & Platform Engineering** - Building the foundation for teams to ship faster
- **System Design** - Patterns and principles for distributed systems
- **Lessons Learned** - Real-world experiences and takeaways

## Why "Infrastructure"?

Infrastructure is the invisible backbone that enables everything else. It's the systems that run 24/7, the pipelines that deploy code, and the architecture decisions that compound over time.
